    What Is the Love Type Test?

    This is a light self-reflection quiz that looks at how you express feelings, how planned or spontaneous you are about dates and relationships, and how much closeness or independence you prefer. Your result is not a diagnosis or a right answer. Use it as a simple way to notice your relationship habits.

    3 Dimensions

    E / R

    Expressive vs Reserved. How you show affection through words and actions.

    P / S

    Planner vs Spontaneous. How you handle dates and the flow of a relationship.

    C / F

    Close vs Independent. How you feel about shared time and personal time.
