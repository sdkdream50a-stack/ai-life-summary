💡 About Compatibility

Our AI compatibility test analyzes birth dates to calculate relationship harmony across five key dimensions: Communication, Values, Energy, Emotional Connection, and Growth Potential. Each aspect contributes to the overall compatibility score.

※ This test is for entertainment purposes only and is not a scientific measurement.

What is AI Compatibility Test?

The AI Compatibility Test is a fun and engaging tool that analyzes the potential harmony between two people based on their birth dates. Our algorithm examines numerical patterns and generates personalized insights about relationship dynamics across multiple dimensions.

Unlike simple zodiac matching, our test evaluates five key compatibility areas: communication style, core values alignment, energy matching, emotional connection depth, and growth potential as a couple. This multi-dimensional approach provides a more nuanced view of relationship compatibility.

With over 500,000 couples tested worldwide, the AI Compatibility Test has become a popular way for partners to spark meaningful conversations about their relationship, for friends to have fun comparing matches, and for curious individuals to explore the potential of new connections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the AI Compatibility Test

How accurate is the compatibility score? ▼

Our compatibility test is designed for entertainment and reflection, not scientific prediction. While the algorithm analyzes meaningful patterns, real relationship success depends on countless factors including effort, communication, and mutual respect. Use the results as a conversation starter, not a definitive judgment!

What if we get a low compatibility score? ▼

A lower score doesn't mean your relationship is doomed! Many successful couples have different personalities that complement each other beautifully. The detailed breakdown shows where you might face challenges, which can actually help you focus on areas for growth. Some of the best relationships are between very different people!

Can I test compatibility with a friend or family member? ▼

Absolutely! While we frame it as a romantic compatibility test, the dimensions we analyze (communication, values, energy, etc.) apply to any close relationship. Many users test with friends, siblings, or colleagues just for fun. The animal couple types are especially amusing in non-romantic contexts!

Is my and my partner's information stored? ▼

No. All compatibility calculations happen locally in your browser. We don't collect, store, or share any birth dates or names entered. Your privacy and your partner's privacy are completely protected.

How do I share results with my partner? ▼

After receiving your results, you can generate beautiful shareable images perfect for Instagram, Twitter, or messaging apps. You can also copy a special link to send to your partner so they can see the results directly. It's a fun way to share and discuss your compatibility!

What languages are supported? ▼

The AI Compatibility Test supports five languages: English, Korean (한국어), Japanese (日本語), Chinese (中文), and Spanish (Español). Each version has been carefully localized to ensure meaningful, culturally appropriate results.
