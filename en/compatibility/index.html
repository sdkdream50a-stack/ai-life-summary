About This AI Compatibility Test

The AI Compatibility Test is a free, entertainment-focused tool that evaluates the romantic potential between two people based on their birthdays. Drawing from established personality and relationship psychology research (including attachment theory and the Big Five traits), the test produces a compatibility score, animal couple type, lucky date suggestion, and a romantic-movie-genre match in seconds. Available in five languages. All processing happens in your browser. Neither birthday is sent to our servers, stored, or shared with third parties. The test is gender-neutral and welcomes any couple type. Treat the compatibility score as one creative perspective among many — not a relationship predictor or relationship advice. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the compatibility test really free?

Yes — completely free, no signup or credit card required. Supported by non-intrusive Google AdSense advertising.

Will both birthdays be saved or shared?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Neither birthday is sent to our servers, stored, or shared with third parties.

Can same-sex or non-binary couples use this test?

Yes. The test is fully gender-neutral and welcomes any couple type. The algorithm reads birthdays without assuming gender or relationship structure.

What does a low compatibility score mean?

It is purely entertainment. A low score does not predict relationship outcomes — many factors not measured here matter far more (communication, shared values, life context). Treat scores as conversation starters, not verdicts.

Why does the same partner give different scores with different friends?

Because compatibility is a pairwise calculation: it measures the interaction between two specific birthday profiles. Both inputs change the output. This is consistent with established relationship research showing fit is contextual.
