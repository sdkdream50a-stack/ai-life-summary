Dr. Kim Minsu is a personality psychology expert with over 10 years of research experience at Seoul National University. Specializing in the Big Five personality model, attachment theory, and neuroplasticity, he brings rigorous scientific methodology to AI Test Lab's psychology content. His research focuses on making complex psychological concepts accessible while maintaining scientific accuracy.
